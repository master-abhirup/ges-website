About The Summit

The Global Entrepreneurship Summit (GES) is IIT Kharagpur's flagship international corporate summit conducted by the Entrepreneurship Cell. As one of Asia's largest student-run entrepreneurial summits, it serves as a dynamic nexus where thought leaders, venture capitalists, policymakers, and emerging innovators converge to network, pitch, and collaborate towards shaping the future of global business.

Who Is It For?

For Startups & Founders

Gain access to crucial seed funding, exclusive mentorship from industry veterans, and the opportunity to showcase your product to a global audience of top-tier Angel Investors and VC firms.

For Students & Innovators

Participate in world-class hackathons, secure premium internships through our Intern Carnival, and interact 1-on-1 with unicorn founders to ignite your entrepreneurial journey.

For Investors & VCs

Scout high-potential, deeply vetted startups emerging from India's premier tech institutions. Engage in closed-door networking dinners with fellow high-net-worth individuals and fund managers.

For Corporate Professionals

Stay ahead of the curve through expert-led workshops on GenAI, product leadership, and market strategies. Build powerful strategic partnerships and drive corporate innovation.

Why Attend GES?

🤝 Networking
